# HelpBook
**Project Introduction**

House workers, elderly caregivers, and home nannies represent an often-overlooked yet powerful segment of the national workforce. In times of national celebrations, natural disasters, or large-scale governmental projects, their adaptability and service-oriented skills can play a vital role in sustaining essential operations. This personal project aims to integrate these professions into a structured retention, repurposing, and empowerment system >> transforming how society views and supports them. Rather than being treated as temporary or low-tier jobs, these roles can become pillars of community resilience and socio-economic contribution.
